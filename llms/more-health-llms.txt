# MORE Health

> MORE Health, Inc. is a global digital health company headquartered in San Mateo, California, that
> provides expert medical second opinions and cross-border telemedicine through a proprietary,
> cloud-based Physician Collaboration Platform. A patient's treating physician and an expert
> specialist jointly review medical history, hospital records, lab results, DICOM imaging, pathology
> imaging and genetic testing, then co-develop a diagnosis and treatment plan over HIPAA-compliant
> video conferencing with translation in 129 languages.
